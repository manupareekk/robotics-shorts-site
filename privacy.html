Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Robotics Shorts Poster ("the app") is a personal tool that uploads its owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

  • Data accessed: OAuth tokens for the owner's accounts, and basic account information (TikTok user.info.basic) needed to publish videos.
  • Use: only to upload and publish the owner's videos, and to check their publish status.
  • Storage: tokens are stored encrypted as secrets and never shared. Only the IDs of published videos are logged.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Other users: the app does not collect any data about other users.
  • Deletion: the owner can revoke access at any time in Google Account settings or TikTok settings. Revoking access deletes the tokens' validity.

The app's use of YouTube data follows the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube API Services Terms.
